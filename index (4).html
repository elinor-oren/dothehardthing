sound familiar

You know that hard thing…

The alarm goes at seven, you're still horizontal at ten, and the day's gone before it started. The email that's been sitting unread for nine days. The form open in a tab since Tuesday. The grant report you keep almost starting.

It's not that you can't, it's that the thing has grown an ugh field, and now even glancing at it makes you flinch. Doing it alone, right now, feels impossible, and the dread compounds every day you don't.

what actually happens

A 95-minute session, roughly.

  1. 0–5 min

    Pick the one thing

    We name it concretely. Not "work on applications," but "submit the form that's been open since Tuesday."

  2. 5–10 min

    Find the first move

    We decide what "done for today" looks like and agree on your very first action, so you can't hide behind vagueness.

  3. 10–90 min

    Do it, with me here

    One long push, or two sprints with a break. I keep you on task and check in so you don't freeze: "read me the sentence you're stuck on," or "write the ugly version first."

  4. 90–95 min

    Wrap up

    We note what got done, celebrate your little win, and set the next step so the momentum carries. If you want, we book the next session, or talk about how to not get stuck on this one again.

I prod. I nag a little. Mostly I make sure the thing gets done.

what people actually leave with

Call it accountability, if you like.

Tools like Focusmate are great, but they only keep you company. I make sure you actually do the thing, and I can usually tell when you're stuck on the task or stuck on the fear underneath it. So far that's meant helping people:

  • get their sleep schedule back under control
  • finally commit to ordering the hair-loss treatment they kept putting off (the foam was never getting used)
  • buy shoes that weren't falling apart, and socks that actually match
  • message the girl, with an actual date idea (not just "hey")
  • cancel the subscriptions they weren't using and divert the money to charity
  • claim back over £25,000 in expenses and unpaid salary
